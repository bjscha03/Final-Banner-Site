## Homepage sequencing and expiration behavior

- Campaigns remain ineligible unless they are explicitly `ready` and include both final desktop and mobile artwork.
- When approved campaign windows overlap, the homepage displays the eligible campaign with the nearest expiration date. This preserves the roadmap windows while creating automatic handoffs: Halloween through October 31, Veterans Day November 1–11, and Thanksgiving November 12–26.
- Each campaign expires at the end of its configured date in `America/New_York`.
- When no approved campaign is active, the existing evergreen hero is returned automatically.
- Back to School expires after August 24. Halloween begins August 25 so the homepage moves directly into the next relevant campaign without a stale back-to-school period.

## Halloween Events & Promotions

**Audience:** retailers, schools, churches, attractions, and community groups preparing trunk-or-treats, festivals, and October promotions.

**Strategy:** Lead with a daylight, family-friendly event scene. Sell practical visibility—event discovery, traffic flow, and readable dates—rather than generic spooky decoration.

**Homepage copy**

- Eyebrow: `Halloween events · Trunk-or-treat · Fall promotions`
- Headline: `Make every fall event easy to find.`
- Description: `Welcome families, guide traffic, and promote October events with bright custom banners and yard signs.`
- Primary CTA: `Design Halloween signage` → `/design`
- Secondary CTA: `Explore vinyl banners` → `/vinyl-banners`

**Supporting modules**

1. `Trunk-or-treat & fall festivals` — Put the event name, date, and time where families can read them before they arrive.
2. `Parking & event directions` — Use matching yard signs to guide cars, mark entrances, and keep activity areas clear.
3. `October business promotions` — Make seasonal sales, attractions, and special hours visible from the street.

**SEO opportunity:** Build `/blog/halloween-event-signage-ideas` around `trunk or treat banner`, `Halloween event signage`, `fall festival banner`, and `Halloween yard signs`. The page should use real planning value: message hierarchy, distance legibility, entrance/parking sign placement, weather-resistant mounting, and an event-sign checklist. Suggested title: `Halloween Event Signage Ideas: Banners & Signs That Guide More Guests`. Internally link to `/design`, `/vinyl-banners`, and `/yard-signs`.

**Artwork spec and final prompt set:** Photorealistic daylight entrance at fictional Maple Hollow Community Center. Original maple-leaf-and-pumpkin identity in burnt orange, deep plum, warm cream, and teal. Finished hemmed vinyl with evenly spaced grommets and believable railing mounts. Verbatim banner text: `TRUNK OR TREAT`, `OCTOBER 31 • 5–8 PM`, `MAPLE HOLLOW COMMUNITY CENTER`. Desktop is a wide product-first 16:10 composition; mobile is a separately generated 4:5 product-first composition. Avoid dark horror imagery, paper-poster appearance, excessive decoration, blocked text, trademarks, watermarks, and BOF branding inside the customer example.

## Veterans Day & Community Recognition

**Audience:** schools, municipalities, nonprofits, veteran organizations, and community event organizers.

**Strategy:** Center respectful public recognition and clear ceremony logistics. Avoid combat, funerary, partisan, or Memorial Day language.

**Homepage copy**

- Eyebrow: `Veterans Day · Ceremonies · Community recognition`
- Headline: `Create a tribute your community can see.`
- Description: `Honor those who served at schools, ceremonies, parades, and community gatherings with clear, respectful custom signage.`
- Primary CTA: `Create a recognition banner` → `/design`
- Secondary CTA: `Explore vinyl banners` → `/vinyl-banners`

**Supporting modules**

1. `Ceremonies & parades` — Create a dignified focal banner for municipal observances, assemblies, and parade routes.
2. `School & community tributes` — Recognize service with readable welcome, appreciation, and event banners.
3. `Arrival & viewing directions` — Mark parking, entrances, ceremony areas, and route changes with matching yard signs.

**SEO opportunity:** Build `/blog/veterans-day-banner-ideas` around `Veterans Day banner`, `Veterans Day parade banner`, `recognition banner`, and `Veterans Day ceremony signs`. Differentiate Veterans Day from Memorial Day, provide respectful wording examples, explain photo/name permission for individual recognition, and include parade/ceremony mounting guidance. Suggested title: `Veterans Day Banner Ideas for Schools, Parades & Community Ceremonies`.

**Artwork spec and final prompt set:** Photorealistic bright civic plaza for fictional Cedar Grove Veterans Council. Original star-and-laurel identity in navy, restrained crimson, ivory, and muted gold. Finished 13oz vinyl with reinforced hems, metal grommets, white cord, and physically believable parade-barricade mounting. Verbatim banner text: `HONORING ALL WHO SERVED`, `VETERANS DAY • NOVEMBER 11`, `CEDAR GROVE VETERANS COUNCIL`. Separate wide 16:10 desktop and 4:5 mobile compositions. Avoid combat, funerary imagery, incorrect flag treatment, partisan symbols, Memorial Day language, trademarks, watermarks, fake paper, and BOF branding inside the customer example.

## Thanksgiving Food Drives & Fall Events

**Audience:** churches, food banks, schools, restaurants, nonprofits, and community groups.

**Strategy:** Emphasize action and logistics—collection dates, entrances, volunteer flow, and visibility—rather than a generic Thanksgiving table scene.

**Homepage copy**

- Eyebrow: `Food drives · Community dinners · Fall events`
- Headline: `Turn community support into a visible invitation.`
- Description: `Promote collection dates, welcome guests, and guide volunteers with custom banners and signs made for busy community events.`
- Primary CTA: `Create Thanksgiving signage` → `/design`
- Secondary CTA: `Shop yard signs` → `/yard-signs`

**Supporting modules**

1. `Food & coat drives` — Show what to donate, when collections end, and where neighbors should bring items.
2. `Community dinner directions` — Guide guests, volunteers, and deliveries with clear entrance and parking signs.
3. `Seasonal business messages` — Promote holiday hours, pickup areas, catering, and fall offers before Thanksgiving week.

**SEO opportunity:** Build `/blog/thanksgiving-food-drive-signs` around `food drive banner`, `Thanksgiving food drive signs`, `community dinner banner`, and `donation drop-off signs`. Include a donor-facing message hierarchy, a printable collection checklist, recommended directional sign count, and placement examples. Suggested title: `Thanksgiving Food Drive Signs: What to Print, Say & Place`.

**Artwork spec and final prompt set:** Photorealistic bright donation-center exterior for fictional Harvest Bridge Food Pantry. Original wheat-and-bridge identity in forest green, harvest gold, ivory, and russet. Finished vinyl with reinforced hems, metal grommets, weather-safe cord, realistic perspective, and a modest canned-food collection as supporting context. Verbatim banner text: `COMMUNITY FOOD DRIVE`, `NOVEMBER 2–21`, `HARVEST BRIDGE FOOD PANTRY`. Separate wide 16:10 desktop and 4:5 mobile compositions. Avoid turkey mascots, feast scenes, food waste, blocked text, fake paper, orange-brown gloom, trademarks, watermarks, and BOF branding inside the customer example.

## Creative QA result

After the homepage redesign landed, this branch was rebased onto the new full-bleed hero rather than restoring the earlier split-column layout. Campaign artwork and headlines are now fully data-driven; the redesigned orange image wash, condensed uppercase typography, navy CTA, order cutoff/ship/delivery panel, and three-item benefit rail remain intact. The current Back to School hero continues to use its optimized AVIF/WebP sources.

The first redesigned render used the live hero's maximum headline scale for every campaign. That made the longer future headlines increase the desktop hero height and over-crop the customer banner. The campaign headline scale was revised by copy length so all three future heroes retain the redesigned proportions and show substantially more of the finished sign. The conditional supporting modules were also restyled with the redesign's square borders, orange rules, condensed headings, navy links, and light warm background, then placed after the product-selection section.

The accessibility pass found insufficient contrast in the redesigned homepage's existing white-on-bright-orange promo bar, CTA buttons, and delivery badges, plus small orange material labels. Those uses now apply the deeper BOF orange `#C94008` (4.69:1 or better against their paired foreground/background), with `#B93808` hover states. Bright orange remains as a non-text accent.

The revised rendered homepage passed all 12 campaign/device combinations:

| Campaign | 1440×1000 desktop | 1280×800 laptop | 390×844 iPhone | 412×915 Android |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Halloween | Pass | Pass | Pass | Pass |
| Veterans Day | Pass | Pass | Pass | Pass |
| Thanksgiving | Pass | Pass | Pass | Pass |

For every render, the test verified the correct responsive WebP, completed image decode, expected natural dimensions, no horizontal overflow, headline/timer containment, visible primary and secondary CTAs, three supporting cards, absence of accidental internal creative notes, no page exceptions, and no serious or critical WCAG A/AA violations. Human inspection also passed image realism, finished-sign materials, spelling, relevance, product prominence, brightness, uniqueness, safe cropping, responsive composition, and visual continuity with the redesigned site.

The isolated browser cannot reach the repository's pre-existing Google Fonts and Cloudinary URLs because outbound network access is restricted in the test container. Those unrelated remote requests were recorded separately; all new campaign images are local, loaded successfully, and remained between 99 KB and 141 KB. Production build, targeted lint, and all seven campaign-selection tests passed. No production deployment is authorized by this brief.
