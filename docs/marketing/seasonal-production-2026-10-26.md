## Creative QA gate

The branch remains draft-only until campaign selection tests, targeted lint, the production build, and all 16 campaign/device page checks pass. The browser matrix covers desktop 1440×1000, laptop 1280×800, iPhone 390×844, and Android 412×915; it verifies the expected asset, natural dimensions, CTA, three supporting modules, overflow, hero containment, page errors, and serious/critical accessibility violations. It also captures 32 review images: hero and supporting modules for every campaign/device combination.

Any failure in source realism, signage construction, intentional text, mounting, product accuracy, relevance, prominence, crop, brightness, uniqueness, readability, accessibility, or performance blocks the campaign and leaves the approved fallback behavior intact.
