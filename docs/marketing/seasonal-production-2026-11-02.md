## Campaign strategy

- **Audience:** Restaurants, retailers, schools, nonprofits, community organizations, and event venues planning February promotions, fundraisers, dances, dinners, and benefit events.
- **Job to be done:** Make one event or offer immediately understandable from the street, then guide guests to parking, check-in, pickup, or the correct entrance.
- **Creative idea:** Show a real finished fundraiser banner at a bright community venue. The fictional Kindred Table Community Kitchen identity feels warm and credible without turning the campaign into generic Valentine's clip art.
- **Message hierarchy:** Organization, event, exact date and time, then supporting wayfinding.
- **Product focus:** Vinyl banners first, with yard signs as the directional companion.
