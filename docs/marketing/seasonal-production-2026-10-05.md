### Desktop generation prompt

Photorealistic wide ecommerce hero of a bright early-evening terrace at fictional Northline Social Hall. Show a finished 13-ounce matte vinyl banner mounted to a black metal railing with reinforced hems, evenly spaced silver grommets, black zip ties, realistic tension, slight ripples, shadows, and perspective. Customer identity uses midnight blue, champagne gold, ivory, restrained teal, and an original compass-star mark. Exact text only: “NORTHLINE SOCIAL HALL”; “MIDNIGHT ON THE TERRACE”; “NEW YEAR'S EVE • DECEMBER 31 • 8 PM”. Complete unobstructed banner on the right, architectural negative space on the left, tasteful string lights, no generic confetti, balloons, bottles, trademarks, watermarks, or extra text.

### Mobile generation prompt

Separate photorealistic 4:5 mobile composition using the same fictional identity and exact banner text. Frame the complete finished banner near-front and centered with breathing room on every edge. Preserve the reinforced hems, silver grommets, black ties, realistic tension, slight ripples, shadows, and perspective. No cropping, obstruction, extra text, unrelated signage, generic confetti, balloons, bottles, trademarks, watermarks, or fake UI.

The first desktop render was rejected because it inserted an unintended separator in the organization name. A targeted generation edit corrected the brand line to the exact text `NORTHLINE SOCIAL HALL`; the corrected raster was re-inspected before WebP optimization.
