Lexington's public configurator also exposes rush and Saturday charges. These are **not enabled**: a competitor charge does not establish our facility/service eligibility or arrival date. The existing banner rush policy is not automatically applied to new products.

Lexington's homepage was checked again on October 12, 2026: it advertises free overnight shipping on every order, no minimum, to all 50 states. This is a retail offer, not a statement of B2Sign's wholesale freight cost. Per the owner's October 12 instruction, that $0 policy applies to all 19 specialty product families, including all newly proposed sizes and accessories. No specialty shipping-policy confirmation remains outstanding.

Matched configurations with fully priced options and complete approved artwork can proceed through checkout. A missing option price keeps the base price and all known adjustments visible, clearly labeled as incomplete, and preserves free shipping. Only the incomplete configuration remains unavailable for payment; its artwork and options can still be saved. Catalog cards and size/option selectors display published prices directly, and the October 13 proposals fill the remaining configurations while retaining their proposed status. Existing banners, yard signs and car magnets retain their price tables, shipping and quantity-discount eligibility.
