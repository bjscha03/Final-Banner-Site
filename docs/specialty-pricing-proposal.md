**Margin check:** actual collected price minus supplier production, packaging, air freight and payment fees determines contribution. For illustration only, a $349 stand with 30% off collects $244.30. With a $75 freight allowance, a 3% fee allowance and a 35% contribution target, allowable supplier/packing cost is about **$76.47**. If actual costs exceed that, adjust the price or promo eligibility before launch. No supplier margin is claimed as verified.
